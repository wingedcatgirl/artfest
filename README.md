A Balatro mod I made to do mass attacks for Artfight. Because it's awesome, is why.

Like the vast majority of Balatro mods, requires [Lovely](https://github.com/ethangreen-dev/lovely-injector) and [Steamodded](https://github.com/Steamodded/smods).

Compatible with [CardPronouns](https://github.com/real-niacat/CardPronouns).  
Has (minor) gratuitous crossmod with [Playwright](https://github.com/cosmeggo/Playwright). Will definitely gain minor gratuitous crossmod with more mods in future updates. (The plan is a mass attack major update each year lol)
